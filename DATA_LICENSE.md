# Data License

The ResearchBench dataset files are released for non-commercial research use under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC 4.0), unless a particular upstream source requires more restrictive handling. The complete dataset is hosted on Hugging Face at `ankilok/ResearchBench`; this GitHub repository only includes the small `data/tiny/*.jsonl` smoke-test files.

The benchmark records include bibliographic metadata, paper titles, abstracts, extracted research questions, extracted background surveys, extracted inspirations, and extracted hypotheses. Paper titles and abstracts originate from their original publishers and scholarly metadata providers. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of abstracts and other source-derived metadata complies with the applicable publisher, venue, and metadata-provider terms.

If you use this dataset, cite the ResearchBench paper and acknowledge the original scholarly works represented by the benchmark.
